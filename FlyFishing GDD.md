<ins>_**Fly Fishing GDD**_</ins>

<ins>Vision<ins>

When team 6 began brainstorming mechanics, I (Payton) was the primary mind 
behind the mechanic. I wanted to experiment with a type of mechanic that I 
wish to implement into a game at some point in the future. 

The core mechanic is essentially the ability to *"harpoon"* enemies and abduct 
them from their allies before engaging in an aerial 1v1 where you have the advantage. 

In order to simplify this into a different aesthetic that is simpler to understand, 
I offered up the concept as a fishing style minigame. Players would fly around on a 
parasail and control the movement of the boat in front of them, but the harpoon and combat itself would involve the character in the sky behind the boat. 

A basic example of what the game might have looked like:

<ins>Goals<ins>

The basic goals of this project were to answer a primary question as well as
a secondary question. 

* Does a harpoon style combat mechanic create cathartic gameplay with room for expansion?
* Is a good "fishing" game reliant on recreating the realistic elements of fishing? 

The primary question was focused on the origin of the mechanic concept, while the 
later was focused on the theming of the prototype. 

<ins>Mechanics<ins>

The mechanics of *FlyFishing* were intended to be fairly simple. The player would simply control the left and rightward movement of a boat which is attached by rope to the player character. This rope would then create more fun and engaging movement. Beyond this you would aim with a mouse and click to throw the harpoon, then have to engage in some kind of fight with the fish afterwards. 

The mechanics as implemented are A / D for left / right movement of the boat and player, as the rope mechanic was not implemented. The harpoon was similarly simplified to be a simple hitbox that would shoot towards the top of the screen from the player, activated with spacebar.
